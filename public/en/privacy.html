Privacy

What Whenly stores

Only what you type yourself: the poll title, the participants' names, the tapped days and an optional time. Nothing else. No email address, no phone number, no account, no password.

What Whenly does not do

No tracking, no ads, no third-party cookies, no analytics. Your browser locally remembers only your language, your name and which polls you opened, so you do not have to retype anything. That stays on your own device.

Who can see a poll

Anyone with the link. The link is a long random code that cannot be guessed, and poll pages are not indexed by search engines, so share the link only with your group. Anyone with the link can also change or remove names and days; when someone removes a name, a short note stays visible for 30 days so the group knows it was not an app error.

How long, and who decides

You do. Next to the title there is "Delete this poll completely". Click it (and confirm) and the title, all names and all tapped days are gone immediately and for good, for everyone. If nobody deletes it, the poll stays for 12 months after the last activity so you can come back to it. That is not to collect data; after that it is deleted automatically. A removed name disappears for good after 30 days.

Where, and questions

Data is stored on Cloudflare servers. Whenly is open source; the code and any questions go to GitHub.
